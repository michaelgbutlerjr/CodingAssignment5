Scrambled Eggs

https://www.loveandlemons.com/scrambled-eggs-recipe/

Ingredients

  • Eggs
  • Milk or Water
  • Extra Virgin Olive Oil or Butter
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Directions

  • 1. Beat the Eggs until thoroughly combined.
  • 2. Gently preheat pan over medium heat, using either olive oil or a little butter in it.
  • 3. Cook the mixed eggs in the pan over the course of 5 minutes while making sure to fold and stir the eggs with a spatula.
